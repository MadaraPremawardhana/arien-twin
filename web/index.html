Why Arien

The forecast works where the data doesn't exist yet.

Most tools either give you a one-off annual estimate or need years of your own generation history. Arien does neither.

No data required

First-principles physics and public weather forecasts drive the model, so a brand-new array is supported the moment you add it — no waiting for a year of readings.

Walk the array in 3D

Test tilt, orientation and shading in a 3D twin of your array (preview) and watch the yield change.

Lighter on the planet

The physics engine uses up to 60× less compute energy than machine-learning forecasters — matched or beat trained ML baselines in our tests, at a fraction of the energy.

How it works

Three steps from a postcode to a plan.

STEP 01

Tell us where

Enter a location and a few panel details — tilt, orientation, wattage. No site history, no uploads.

STEP 02

We model the physics

Solar geometry, irradiance and live weather forecasts drive a digital twin of your exact system, hour by hour.

STEP 03

You get answers

A 7-day hourly forecast, plain-English recommendations, and an interactive 3D twin you can experiment in.
