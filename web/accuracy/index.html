Accuracy

Validated against real rooftops

Validated against measured rooftop generation at two UK sites: Knorr-Bremse (Melksham) and Belloff House, University of Buckingham. Forecasts driven by weather forecasts issued 1–7 days ahead, Jan–May 2025 (1,057 forecast-day pairs per model).

Limitations

Both validation sites are in the UK. Soiling, degradation and micro-cracks are not modelled yet. Accuracy falls beyond 4–5 days as weather forecasts lose skill.
