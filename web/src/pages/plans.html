FAQ

Questions, answered

What data do you store?

Only what you give us: your account details, the sites you design, and any settings you save. Site geometry is stored so your dashboard and forecasts can reload it, and you can download or delete everything at any time from Settings.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There's no lock-in and no minimum term — change or leave a plan whenever you like from Settings.

How is academic access verified?

Apply with an institutional email address. Addresses on recognised academic domains (.ac.uk, .edu, .edu.au, .ac.lk, .ac.in, .ac.nz, .ac.za) are approved automatically; everything else is reviewed by our team, usually within 2 working days.

How accurate is the forecast?

Our physics model achieves a mean absolute error of 21 Wh at one day ahead, measured across 1,057 forecast-day pairs on real UK rooftops. See the full validation →
